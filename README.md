# Topline Odds - trial-outcome calibration

This file lists every forecast published in a sent issue of Topline Odds and of its castellano edition, Probabilidad Clinica.

Each line gives the forecast date, the company with its ticker as an identifier, the trial, its registry ids, the indication, the phase, the primary endpoint, our probability that the trial meets its primary endpoint, the result, the Brier score, the grading date and the source of the timing sentence.

A probability here is our estimate that a trial meets its primary endpoint. It is not a forecast of any share price, and the two often differ.

A forecast is never edited after publication. Every forecast stays on the list, whether the primary endpoint was met or not. The result column reads met, not met, no result or open.

When a published figure proves wrong, the next issue opens with a correction line and the archive post gets a dated edit note. A correction never changes a logged probability.
